🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective: January 1, 2026 Last Updated: June 2026 Version 2.1

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Rise ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use our job board platform. By using the Service, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

📌 Quick Summary: We collect minimal data to provide and improve our job board service. We do not sell your data. We only share data with third parties when necessary for service delivery or as required by law.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when using the Service:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password (encrypted) when you create an account
  • Profile Information: Job preferences, skills, location, phone number, bio
  • Saved Jobs: Jobs you bookmark or save for later
  • Referral Codes: Referral codes you may enter during registration
  • Communications: Messages you send to us for support or inquiries

1.2 Information Automatically Collected

When you use the Service, we automatically collect certain information:

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, jobs viewed, search queries, clicks, and interactions
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device type, screen resolution
  • IP Address: Your IP address for analytics and security purposes
  • Cookies: Small text files stored on your device (see Section 6)

1.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from:

  • Social Media: If you sign in via Google or Facebook, we receive your name and email address
  • Analytics Providers: We use services like Google Analytics to understand usage patterns

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Personalization: To recommend relevant jobs and customize your experience
  • Account Management: To create and manage your account, and to communicate with you
  • Notifications: To send you job alerts, application updates, and other relevant notifications
  • Analytics: To analyze usage patterns, improve features, and measure performance
  • Security: To protect against fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Compliance: To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following situations:

3.1 Service Providers

We share data with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating the Service:

  • Hosting & Infrastructure: Cloud providers (e.g., AWS) that store and process data
  • Analytics: Analytics providers (e.g., Google Analytics) to understand usage
  • Authentication: Social login providers (Google, Facebook) for authentication

3.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental regulation, or if we believe such disclosure is necessary to protect our rights or the safety of others.

3.3 Business Transfers

If Rise is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. We may also retain and use your information as necessary to:

  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce our agreements
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud

If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required by law.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
  • Access Controls: Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel
  • Regular Audits: We regularly review our security practices
  • Authentication: Secure password hashing and authentication protocols

While we take reasonable measures, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience:

Type Purpose Duration
Essential Required for basic functionality (session management, authentication) Session / Persistent
Preferences Remember your preferences (language, theme, filters) Persistent (up to 1 year)
Analytics Understand how you use the Service (Google Analytics) Persistent (up to 2 years)
Authentication Keep you logged in (session tokens) Session / Persistent

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect certain features.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of marketing communications and certain data processing
  • Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for processing based on consent

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@rise.app. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Third-Party Links

The Service contains links to third-party websites, including employer application pages. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external sites you visit.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us, and we will delete it.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. We take appropriate measures to ensure that such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the Service after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@rise.app
  • Address: Rise Inc., 123 Tech Park, Suite 400, Wilmington, DE 19801

🔐 Your privacy is important to us. We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about our practices.
