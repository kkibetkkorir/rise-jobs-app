❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for a job? ▼
Simply click the "Apply →" button on any job card. This will take you to a detailed job page where you can read the full description. Click "Apply now" to be redirected to the employer's official application page where you can submit your application.
Is Rise free to use? ▼
Yes! Rise is completely free for job seekers. We believe that finding your next opportunity shouldn't cost anything. We also offer free job listings for employers through our API partners.
How do I save a job? ▼
You need to create an account first. Once logged in, you'll see a "Save" button on each job card. Click it to add the job to your saved list. You can access your saved jobs from your account dashboard.
How do I report a problem with a job listing? ▼
If you notice an issue with a job listing (e.g., broken link, incorrect information), please contact us using the form above or email us at hello@rise.app with the job title and company name.
How do I delete my account? ▼
You can delete your account from the Settings tab in your account dashboard. Click "Delete Account" and confirm the action. All your data will be permanently removed from our system.
