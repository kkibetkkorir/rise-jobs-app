👋 How to apply for jobs

A simple 3-step guide to finding and applying for your next role
🔍

1. Browse & search for jobs

Start on the job board where you'll see all available positions. You can:

  • 🔎 Search by job title, company, or location
  • 🏷️ Filter by work type (Hybrid, Remote) or category (Startup, Legal, Growth, Operations)
  • 📄 Browse through pages of job listings
🖥️ Job cards show company name, logo, title, location, and job type at a glance.
💡 Tip: Use the search bar to find specific roles quickly
👆

2. Click "Apply →" on any job

Found a job that interests you? Click the "Apply →" button on the job card. This will take you to a detailed job page where you can:

  • 📖 Read the full job description
  • 🏢 Learn more about the company
  • 📍 See the exact location and work arrangement
  • 📋 Review requirements and responsibilities
⚡ You're not applying yet! This page just shows you all the details so you can make an informed decision.
📌 The job ID is passed automatically — you don't need to do anything
🚀

3. Apply on the company's website

After reviewing the job details, click the big "Apply now" button. This will open the official application page on the company's career site (e.g., Lever, Greenhouse, SmartRecruiters, etc.) in a new tab.

  • 📝 Fill out the application form
  • 📎 Upload your resume and cover letter
  • ✅ Submit your application directly to the employer
🌐 You'll be redirected to the company's own application system to complete your submission.
🔒 Your information goes directly to the employer — we never store your application data

📌 Quick recap

1. Browse jobs → 2. Click "Apply →" → 3. Read details → 4. Click "Apply now" → 5. Apply on employer site

❓ Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No! You don't need to sign up or create any account. The job board is completely open and free to use.

Where does the job data come from?

All jobs are sourced directly from the Rise API, which aggregates listings from thousands of companies worldwide.

Is my application data stored?

No. When you click "Apply now," you're redirected to the employer's website. We never store or process your application data.

Can I save jobs for later?

Currently, the job board is for browsing only. We recommend bookmarking interesting jobs or keeping a list of your favorites.
