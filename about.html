About Rise

Connecting talent with opportunity — simply, transparently, and for free.

Our Mission

At Rise, we believe that finding your next career opportunity should be simple, accessible, and transparent. Our mission is to connect job seekers with meaningful opportunities while providing employers with a platform to reach the right talent.

✨ What makes Rise different? We're a free, open job board that aggregates listings from thousands of companies worldwide. No hidden fees, no paywalls, no gimmicks — just quality job listings in one place.

What We Offer

  • 🔍 Comprehensive Job Search: Browse thousands of jobs from companies of all sizes, from startups to Fortune 500s.
  • 🏷️ Smart Filters: Find exactly what you're looking for with filters for job type, location, company size, and more.
  • 💾 Save & Track: Save interesting jobs, track your applications, and get notified about new opportunities.
  • 📱 Responsive Design: Search and apply from any device — desktop, tablet, or mobile.
  • 🔗 Direct Applications: Apply directly on employer websites — we never store your application data.

How It Works

  1. Browse: Search through thousands of job listings using filters and keywords.
  2. Discover: Click on any job to see the full description, company details, and requirements.
  3. Apply: Click "Apply now" to be redirected to the employer's official application page.
  4. Track: Create an account to save jobs, receive notifications, and manage your applications.

Our Technology

Rise is powered by the Rise API, which aggregates job listings from thousands of sources. We use modern web technologies to deliver a fast, responsive, and secure experience. Our platform is built with privacy and security in mind, with encryption for all data in transit and at rest.
